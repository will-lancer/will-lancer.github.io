  • h/2e Flux Trapping without Superconductivity

    Will Lancer

    Advised by Zohar Komargodski and Fedor Popov

    • Flux trapping
    • Aharonov–Bohm effect
    • Normal metals
    • Finite temperature

    Recently, it was shown by Komargodski and Popov that ordinary metals threaded with an Aharonov–Bohm solenoid exclusively trap either 0 or h/2e flux. The latter value is familiar in the superconducting setting, yet we find that it holds in ordinary metals as well, with no condensate or pairing necessary. We provide an analytic explanation of the flux trapping, and extend the results to the case of finite temperature and time-reversal invariant potentials. We also find that the flux remains trapped at 0 and h/2e for finite temperature and for lattice regularization.

  • Symmetry Transmutation from RG Wall Correspondences

    Codex

    • Generalized symmetry
    • RG interface
    • Tube algebra
    • Differential cohomology

    Follows. Seiberg and Seifnashri, Symmetry Transmutation and Anomaly Matching, especially §6.2, §6.3, and §7.

    They replace the usual UV-to-IR group homomorphism,

    \varphi\colon G_{\mathrm{UV}}\longrightarrow G_{\mathrm{IR}}

    by a map of background fields:

    B_{\mathrm{IR}}=\Phi(A_{\mathrm{UV}}).

    For discrete invertible 0-form to 1-form symmetries, they classify \Phi by a central extension, equivalently H^2(G,K), and they identify that with a map of classifying spaces of defect networks. They then leave three things unformulated as one object: \Phi as a higher homomorphism; the noninvertible case, where they say \Phi should be a map of tube algebras because there are no ordinary background gauge fields; and continuous symmetries with nonflat backgrounds (the FQHE formula B=\mathrm{d}A), which topological defect networks do not capture.

    The draft defines a single background stack with connections on top-dimensional strata and (possibly noninvertible) defects on lower strata. An RG wall supplies a kernel of junction spaces. If that kernel is representable on the transmitted IR sector, Yoneda produces a symmetric-monoidal map \Phi, unique up to contractible choice; right-principality of the IR wall action is a sufficient condition. Restrictions of this one map recover the classifying-space map, including the central-extension obstruction BG\to B^2K; a tube-algebra homomorphism, by annularizing a strong unitary pivotal tensor functor on codimension-one defects; and a morphism of connection stacks that keeps curvature and gauge descent. Anomaly matching is separate: it needs a local Wilsonian factorization with a pointwise-nonzero determinant-line map, after which the UV anomaly is the pulled-back IR anomaly tensored with the wall contribution. The note does not construct the wall for any specific RG flow; a concrete theory still has to supply that representation.

  • Tangential A_4 Background Laws at Fixed Calibration

    Codex

    • Lattice gauge theory
    • Group cohomology
    • Symmetry fractionalization
    • A_4

    Follows. Seiberg and Seifnashri, Symmetry Transmutation and Anomaly Matching, Appendix B.

    For ordinary 0-form to p-form transmutation they classify the background map by H^{p+1}(BG,K). Appendix B then says there should also be maps that depend on tangent-bundle characteristic classes (Stiefel–Whitney classes of TM) and therefore do not factor through BG alone.

    This note does not construct such a transmutation. It classifies simultaneous A_4 0-form and calibrated \mathbb{Z}_2^{(1)} symmetries in one 1+1d bosonic lattice gauge theory on unoriented O(2) surfaces. Both symmetries are present at every scale, so the graph is fractionalization data, not a UV-to-IR transmutation. Inside the class of finite-depth source-covariant conjugates of one gapped Hamiltonian, and for graphs with characteristic descent, the pointed law is classified by the relative mapping 2-type

    \operatorname{Map}\!\left( (BA_4\times BO(2),\{*\}\times BO(2)), (B^2\mathbb{Z}_2,*) \right).

    That type is \{0,\omega\}. Here \omega is the binary-tetrahedral class of

    1\longrightarrow\mathbb{Z}_2\longrightarrow SL(2,3) \longrightarrow A_4\longrightarrow 1.

    The mixed term that would carry the tangent dependence vanishes:

    H^1(BA_4;\mathbb{Z}_2)\otimes H^1(BO(2);\mathbb{Z}_2)=0,

    since H^1(BA_4;\mathbb{Z}_2)=0. The background law is therefore

    B=c\,\omega(A),\qquad c\in\mathbb{Z}_2.

    On \mathbb{RP}^2, one can turn on the two classes at once:

    \omega(A)\neq 0,\qquad w_1^2=w_2\neq 0.

    A finite-coupling partition function still only sees the ordinary component. The dynamical transmutation version of the problem is left open: that would need a model whose symmetry content actually changes with scale.

  • Large-Spin CFTs on Plane Waves

    Codex

    • Large-spin CFT
    • Plane waves
    • Heisenberg symmetry
    • Kerr–AdS

    Follows. Komargodski, Miscioscia, and Popov, Regge's Inferno, especially §1.2.

    KMP show that the large-spin limits of a CFT on the Lorentzian cylinder, taking J_z\to\infty at fixed \tau/\sqrt{J_z} in three dimensions, or J_1,J_2\to\infty at fixed J_1/J_2 and \tau/(J_1J_2)^{1/3} in four, are governed by plane waves with an emergent Heisenberg symmetry. They derive the universal extensive thermodynamics from that symmetry alone and list five calculations it leaves open: correlators, thermal nonanalyticity, causality and positive energy, supersymmetry and indices, and a large-N gravity dual with pp-wave boundary conditions.

    This note works through all five. Heisenberg symmetry alone fixes the vacuum two-point function transported to the pp wave as a Mehler kernel,

    \widetilde G_p(T)= {2\pi C_\mathcal{O} e^{i\pi\Delta/2}\over\Gamma(\Delta)} {\Theta(p\sin T)|p|^{\Delta-1}\over|\sin T|^\Delta} \exp\!\left[-ip\,{(|z_1|^2+|z_2|^2)\cos T-2z_1\cdot z_2\over\sin T}\right],

    and fixes the selection rules and orbit dependence of heavy-state correlators, though the reduced OPE matrices they act on remain theory-specific data. Taking the angular velocities of Kerr–AdS to the speed of light gives the boundary pressures

    \mathcal F_3(r)={(r^2-1)^2\over16\pi G_4 r},\qquad \mathcal F_4(r)={(r^2-1)^3\over16\pi G_5 r^2},

    which are non-analytic at T_c=1/(2\pi), with second- and third-order Ehrenfest transitions respectively; their Legendre–Fenchel transforms give the large-spin entropy functions. The pp waves are stably causal but not globally hyperbolic, and the conformal net inherited from the cylinder is microcausal with a self-adjoint time generator; positivity of the double-spin Hamiltonian reduces, case by case, to the all-representation bound \Delta\geq\max(k,\bar k), which is left as a conjecture. For a chosen superconformal algebra and R-charge ray, the surviving superalgebra is identified as the zero-grade centralizer of the contraction. Finally, the note exhibits exact Kerr–AdS_4 and Kerr–AdS_5 metrics whose conformal boundaries are exactly the KMP pp waves, giving the large-N gravity duals KMP ask for.
