  • h/2e Flux Trapping without Superconductivity

    Recently, it was shown by Komargodski and Popov that ordinary metals threaded with an Aharonov–Bohm solenoid exclusively trap either 0 or h/2e flux. The latter value is familiar in the superconducting setting, yet we find that it holds in ordinary metals as well, with no condensate or pairing necessary. We provide an analytic explanation of the flux trapping, and extend the results to the case of finite temperature and time-reversal invariant potentials. We also find that the flux remains trapped at 0 and h/2e for finite temperature and for lattice regularization.
