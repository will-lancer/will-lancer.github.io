Why effective theories work so well

August 19, 2026 at 9:17:36 AM MST · 5 min read

There is a useful way to think about an effective field theory: it is a disciplined refusal to claim knowledge of short distances. We write every interaction allowed by the symmetries and order the terms by how strongly they can matter at the scale of the experiment.

For a scalar field, the beginning of the expansion might look like

\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{eff}} = \frac{1}{2}(\partial \phi)^2 - \frac{m^2}{2}\phi^2 - \frac{\lambda}{4!}\phi^4 + \sum_{n>4}\frac{c_n}{\Lambda^{n-4}}\mathcal{O}_n.

The parameter \Lambda records where the description should stop being trusted. At energies E \ll \Lambda, operators with large n are suppressed by powers of E/\Lambda. The theory becomes predictive because ignorance arrives in an ordered series.

This is more than a calculational convenience. It tells us which questions can be answered without knowing the microscopic theory. Low-energy regularities can be stable even when the ultraviolet story changes completely.

The same pattern appears elsewhere in mathematics and computation. A useful abstraction preserves the terms that control the behavior at the scale we care about. Everything else enters as a bounded correction.

What I want from a formalization

August 17, 2026 at 2:42:08 PM MST · 4 min read

A formal proof becomes interesting when it exposes the load-bearing structure of an argument. The target is a transcription of symbols into a proof assistant. The result should make its assumptions, witnesses, and limiting procedures impossible to blur.

For a convergence claim, I want to see the quantifiers in their natural order:

\forall \varepsilon > 0,\; \exists N,\; \forall n \geq N,\quad \lVert f_n-f\rVert < \varepsilon.

Changing that order changes the theorem. So does hiding a compactness assumption inside a helper lemma. Formalization is most valuable at precisely those seams.

My working test is simple. If the formal statement can be true while the paper's intended theorem is false, the contract is still incomplete.
